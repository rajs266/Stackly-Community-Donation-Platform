How Community Solidarity Saved 450 Children From Malnutrition

In July 2026, Stackly initiated the Salem Morning Nutrition Drive targeting informal settlements near Chinna Thirupathi. Thanks to micro-donations of ₹500 and ₹1,000 from working professionals across Tamil Nadu, 450 school-going children received balanced breakfasts rich in sprouts, milk, boiled eggs, and pulses.

"School attendance rose by 34% within 60 days of starting the breakfast program," reports Headmaster Murugesan. Every grain and vegetable was procured from local Salem farmers' markets, creating a secondary income loop for small cultivators.
